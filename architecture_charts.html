🔄 3. End-to-End Campaign Data Lifecycle
1
Bulk ECI Roll Import
Official voter lists (CSV/Excel) are streamed into the global master database in 5,000-row batch chunks.
2
Campaign Provisioning
A new Campaign Workspace is created and assigned a target Assembly Constituency or set of Polling Booths.
3
Worker Allocation
Booth Managers & Field Canvassers are linked to the Campaign with RBAC role permissions.
4
Door-to-Door Canvassing
Field workers collect mobile numbers, household structures, and voter inclination (Pro/Neutral/Opposed).
5
Isolated Data Storage
Canvassing notes and contact data are saved under `campaign_voter_details`, 100% private to that campaign.
6
Real-Time Analytics
Live dashboard charts display favorable voter targeting, booth turnout stats, and GOTV lists.
